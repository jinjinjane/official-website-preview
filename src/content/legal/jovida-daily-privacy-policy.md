## 1. INTRODUCTION

Welcome to Jovida, a task management and productivity companion service operated by Fluxvita Corporation ("Fluxvita," "we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use our services through our mobile application ("App"), website at jovida.ai ("Website"), and other communication channels including WhatsApp, iMessage, SMS, and email (collectively, the "Services").

**Our Commitment to Your Privacy:**
- We do NOT sell your personal data to third parties
- We do NOT use your data for advertising or marketing purposes
- We use your data solely to provide and improve our AI companion services

By using our Services, you agree to the collection, use, and disclosure of your information as described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our Services.

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland: Fluxvita Corporation is the **data controller** responsible for your personal data. This Privacy Policy should be read together with our Terms and Conditions.

## 2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

### 2.1 Personal Information You Provide

**Account Information:**
- Name (first and last)
- Email address
- Phone number
- Date of birth

**Communications:**
- Messages and conversations with our AI companion through any channel (App, WhatsApp, iMessage, SMS, email)
- Voice messages and recordings (when you use voice features)
- Customer support inquiries and correspondence
- Feedback and survey responses

**Payment Information:**
- Payment card details (processed by third-party payment processors)
- Billing address
- Transaction history

### 2.2 Automatically Collected Information

**Device and Usage Information:**
- Device type, model, and operating system
- IP address and general location (city/state level)
- Browser type and version
- App version and settings
- Pages or screens viewed and time spent
- Features used and interaction patterns
- Date and time of access

**Cookies and Tracking Technologies:**
- Cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies (see Section 9)

- Analytics data

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information from:
- Payment processors (transaction confirmation)
- Third-party authentication services (e.g., Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In)

### 2.4 Voice Data

When you use voice features to communicate with our AI companion, we may process voice recordings.

**How We Use Voice Recordings:**
- Voice recordings are converted to text for AI processing purposes only
- We do not create or retain voice templates, voiceprints, or biometric identifiers
- Voice data is used solely to provide the companion service, not for identification or authentication

**Important Notice (Illinois BIPA and Similar Laws):**  
We do NOT collect biometric identifiers including voiceprints, facial scans, fingerprints, or any other biometric templates used for individual identification. If future biometric features are introduced, we will provide separate written notice and obtain informed consent before collection.

## 7. DATA SECURITY & BREACH NOTIFICATION

### 7.1 Security Measures
We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:
- **Encryption:** Data encrypted in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest
- **Access Controls:** Restricted access on a need-to-know basis
- **Authentication:** Secure login with password requirements
- **Monitoring:** Regular security audits and monitoring for unauthorized access

No security system is impenetrable. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

### 7.2 Data Breach Notification
In the event of a personal data breach:
- We will notify the relevant supervisory authority within **72 hours** of becoming aware of the breach, where required by applicable law (including GDPR Article 33)
- Where a breach is likely to result in high risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify you directly without undue delay (GDPR Article 34)
- Notification will describe the nature of the breach, categories of data affected, likely consequences, and measures taken
